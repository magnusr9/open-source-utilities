# Privacy Policy – MagnusR9 Browser Extensions & Plugins

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Maintainer:** MagnusR9 (`magnusr9.dev@gmail.com`)

## 1. Zero Data Collection & 100% Local Execution
All browser extensions and utilities published by **MagnusR9** (*Media Deduplicator & Broken Image Auditor*, *Copy All URLs, Tab Exporter & Duplicate Closer*, and *Export SVG with Computed Styles & Code Copy*) operate **100% locally within your web browser**.

* We **do not** collect, store, transmit, log, or sell any personal data, browsing history, tab URLs, website content, or user activity.
* Our extensions use **zero external servers**, zero analytics scripts, and zero third-party trackers.

## 2. Permissions Justification
* **`activeTab` & `scripting`:** Used strictly on-demand when you explicitly click the scan/extract button inside the extension popup to inspect DOM elements (`<img>` or `<svg>`) on the currently active page.
* **`tabs`:** Used strictly on-demand in *Copy All URLs & Tab Exporter* to read open tab titles/URLs for clipboard export and duplicate tab closing.
